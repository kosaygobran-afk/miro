# MIRO 0.1.6

Source branch `0.1.6` merges into `main`. The package and lockfile are both version `0.1.6`.

## Changes

- Updated the storefront navbar with gold hover and current-page emphasis, frameless utilities, a bordered search field, and a cleaner Store category dropdown. Headline spacing provides separation on desktop and phones.
- Added public product suggestions in English and Hebrew with up to five results per page. Search matches product names, brand, model, slug, SKU and barcode. Results open the storefront product page and follow public catalog visibility rules.
- Restored the original CEO and admin navbar and workspace finder. The topbar background matches the management shell in each theme.
- Aligned the collection intro to the left in both locales while preserving text direction. Kept the selected category chip visible during resizing and gave desktop filters and search separate rows.
- Included the verified Next.js 16.4.6 security upgrade. [Project status and earlier verification](../PROJECT_STATUS.md)

## Storefront and commerce follow-up — 2026-11-23

- **Cart and Wishlist.** Both pages have bilingual headings, icons, item counts, useful empty states and links between them. The Cart separates unit prices, item totals and the estimated subtotal. The Wishlist keeps the standard product cards and explains where selections are stored.
- **Guest and account shopping.** Visitors can add products to either list. Guest selections are stored in the browser and merge into the account on sign-in after server confirmation. Signed-in selections reopen in a new browser. Temporary account read failures keep the browser lists usable and offer a retry.
- **Checkout requests.** Guest and signed-in customers provide a name, email and phone number and choose delivery or pickup. Delivery asks for the shipping address. Signed-in checkout prefills account details. The server verifies the cart against the catalog and records the request and audit event together. Retries return the original confirmation reference.
- **Database rollout.** Applied the account shopping migration and staged the checkout writer cutover so older storefront code could continue accepting requests until production had switched. The final hosted checks confirmed account persistence, service-only checkout writes and an up-to-date migration registry.
- **Commerce documents.** The eight draft templates share updated English and Hebrew layouts for A4 and 80 mm thermal paper. Numeric columns, long addresses, payment details and page continuations were checked in PDFs and browser print. Access remains inside the authorized management preview.
- **Navigation and theme.** About, Contact and the quote shortcut appear in the guest navbar. Signed-in customers can open About us and Contact us from the footer. Dark, Medium and Light selections persist when switching languages, including during a theme transition.

Production storefront checks covered a disposable account, browser-to-account transfer, a fresh browser, pickup prefilling, sign-out, and both desktop and phone navigation. The database verification passed 63 migrations, 30 SQL suites and nine concurrency checks. Document verification exercised 32 combinations across the eight types, two languages and two paper sizes.
