## Changes

- Version 2 grid, moving and related product cards share a joined stock-left/SKU-right band, complete SKU copying, hover/focus overflow rails and honest inventory colors. Barcode stays in the popup/detail. The compact price panel follows product details, with the amount left and localized context right, a labeled real original price and inline savings. Four equal footer cells retain their actions and portaled hints with theme-gold hover/focus. The sampled card is another 42px shorter after the final price/footer pass. Version 1, source photos, true prices/stock and preview/hold behavior remain protected.
- Product-specific complementary and accompanying choices appear below variants on the detail page when configured. CEO/admin editing, atomic revision-aware saves, exact offer prices, separate parent cart configurations, nested cart/checkout lines and current-server enquiry/checkout validation ship together. The configuration has no arbitrary row cap; transactions retain their existing 100 flattened-line limit. Customer/guest idempotency, aggregate stock and immutable quote/order provenance remain enforced.
- Includes the two additive optional-offer migrations and their SQL/concurrency fixtures. They were already applied to the linked database on 2026-11-05, with no owner product options configured for demonstrations. Release packaging does not reset, reseed or reapply the linked database. Read `docs/PRODUCT_OPTIONAL_OFFERS.md` before installing these changes in another environment.
- Public contact refresh failures remain inside the caller's fallback rather than escaping through Next's background revalidation logger. Only successful whitelisted public projections are cached; catalog stock/prices and financial data stay request-scoped.
