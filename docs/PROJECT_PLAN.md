# MIRO Project Plan and Pilot Readiness

Updated 24 November 2026

MIRO has a live bilingual storefront, working Cart and Wishlist, and checkout requests that staff can review. The next milestone is an internal rehearsal followed by a small, invited **request-only pilot**. Customers can browse and send an enquiry or checkout request. MIRO staff confirm availability, the final total, and pickup or delivery directly with the customer. Live payments and official invoices are later milestones.

## Before inviting customers

- [ ] **Business owner and release owner — approve the pilot hosting and URL.** The Vercel team currently reports Hobby, whose terms restrict it to personal or noncommercial use. Choose hosting that permits MIRO's business pilot and verify the pilot URL. The current storefront is publicly reachable. If the pilot must be private, verify access with a guest browser before sharing the URL. [Vercel terms](https://vercel.com/legal/terms) · [Operations](OPERATIONS.md#deployment)
- [ ] **Business and inventory — approve the products and fulfillment offered in the pilot.** Pick a small set of products and services. Confirm the published photos and brand rights, variants, prices and VAT display, stock, warranty claims, service area, contact details, and how staff arrange pickup or delivery. Use the existing quote path where price or availability still needs confirmation. [Catalog workflow](ARCHITECTURE.md#catalog-and-inventory-workflow-2026-10-01)
- [ ] **Business, legal and privacy — approve the Hebrew and English customer pages.** Privacy, Terms and Accessibility currently display draft copy. Approve the business identity and support details, checkout wording, contact and delivery-address retention, account deletion handling, and analytics disclosures before inviting people to enter real details. [Legal checklist](LEGAL_CHECKLIST_IL.md) · [Account operations](OPERATIONS.md#phase-2-account-operations)
- [ ] **Support and engineering — prove that someone receives and handles every request.** Name a primary and backup for Admin → Requests. Rehearse a contact enquiry, guest delivery, signed-in pickup, and checkout retry with disposable test data on the candidate build. Match the checkout reference to one saved request, confirm the details in the queue, and rehearse the reply. For a small pilot, staff can check the queue manually during agreed hours while automated notifications are pending. [Requests and roles](ROUTES_AND_ROLES.md) · [Project status](PROJECT_STATUS.md)
- [ ] **Engineering — prove real inbox delivery if the pilot includes accounts.** Test signup confirmation and password reset using an approved address outside the Supabase team and check that both links return to the pilot URL in the right language. Confirm the custom SMTP setup and redirect allowlist. A guest-only first cohort is an option while this check is open. [Account operations](OPERATIONS.md#phase-2-account-operations) · [Supabase Auth email requirements](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/auth/auth-smtp)
- [ ] **QA and release owner — sign off on the exact pilot build.** Use real iPhone and Android devices, desktop keyboard and zoom, and Hebrew and English screen readers. Complete search, product selection, Cart, Wishlist, contact, and checkout. Confirm the deployed commit and database health, assign a monitor for errors and missed requests, and identify a rollback build compatible with the current checkout writer. [Operations](OPERATIONS.md) · [Project status](PROJECT_STATUS.md)

## Proposed pilot size and success criteria

1. **Rehearse with 2–3 staff or trusted testers.** Use disposable data. On staging, run the browse, search and request flows with five concurrent sessions. Confirm that each accepted checkout has one reference and saved request, retries keep the same reference, and the core flows complete without unexpected server errors. Repeat at ten concurrent sessions before expanding.
2. **Invite five customers for two business days** after the relevant checks above pass. Ask them to find a product, use the Cart or Wishlist, and send a request if they want a follow-up. Cover both languages and mobile and desktop. Record completed tasks, errors, checkout references and response times through the approved support process. Aim to respond within one business day.
3. **Expand to 10–15 customers total for one week** if requests are consistently recorded once and answered on time, stock and prices shown to customers match staff expectations, and the core flows work on the tested devices. Pause invitations for lost or duplicated requests, a privacy issue, incorrect stock or price, or a support backlog. Review the results with the business owner before opening the storefront more widely.

These are proposed cohort sizes and support targets. Check site performance and staff capacity during the rehearsal and pilot before committing to a larger audience.
